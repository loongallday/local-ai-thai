# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Thai SME owners and managers who want private AI installed in their office, without cloud exposure or a separate team of programmers.

## Product Purpose

Sell the Local edition as one usable on-site system containing the AI machine, Mimir Suites, installation, training, and support.

## Brand Personality

Technical, energetic, and reassuring. The site should make a sophisticated local system feel tangible and supported.

## Anti-references

- Cloud-AI privacy language or hidden external processing.
- Generic computer-shop positioning.
- Named pricing tiers that conflict with sizing each deal.
- Copy or visual treatment pasted from sibling Local AI sites.

## Design Principles

- Show the path from company data to useful work.
- Make on-site processing and ownership concrete.
- Keep Thai copy primary with English technical nouns inline.
- Explain the complete delivery system, not parts in isolation.
- Use motion to explain data flow rather than decorate.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA with semantic structure, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, 44px touch targets, and reduced-motion alternatives.
